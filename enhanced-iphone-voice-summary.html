🔤 Punctuation Processing

📝 Processing Rules

Periods (.)
→ 0.8 second pause
Natural sentence ending
Commas (,)
→ 0.5 second pause
Brief clause separation
Questions (?)
→ 0.9 second pause
Longer pause for reflection
Exclamations (!)
→ 0.8 second pause
Emphasis with pause

❌ Before (Raw Text):
